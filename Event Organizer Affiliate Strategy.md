# 🤝 Event Organizer Affiliate Partnership Program

## Strategic Overview

Physical botanical event organizers constantly seek innovative methodologies to boost pre-event attendance metrics and drive early ticket sales. We will convert them into our most potent distribution channel by creating a **mutually beneficial revenue-sharing model**.

---

## The Revenue Engine

### 1. Embeddable Pre-Sale Portal
Major expo organizers (e.g., The BIG Plant Expo, Hortlandia, Rare Plant Fairy) embed the **rareplantvendors.com Early-Access Pre-Sale Portal** directly onto their own event websites. This portal allows collectors to:
- Browse the verified vendor roster for that specific event
- Reserve specific plants before the doors open
- Pay a refundable reservation deposit (held by the platform)

### 2. Revenue-Sharing Model
In exchange for driving massive, highly targeted regional traffic directly to the directory:
- The event organizer receives a **percentage of the platform's reservation deposit fees**
- The organizer gets a **co-branded analytics dashboard** showing pre-sale engagement metrics
- Organizers receive **priority listing and premium event page design** on the platform

### 3. Bundled VIP Package
The directory can seamlessly bundle its **"Premium Collector Pass"** directly with the event's physical VIP tickets during checkout:
- **Unified Digital/Physical VIP Package:** Collectors purchase one package that includes both the event's VIP ticket AND a Premium Collector subscription
- **Zero Customer Acquisition Cost:** Thousands of new users are funneled into the platform's ecosystem automatically
- **Instant Activation:** The collector's Premium status activates immediately upon ticket purchase

| Pain Point | Our Solution |
