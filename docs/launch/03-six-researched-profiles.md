Date: 2026-10-24
Status: implemented on the review branch; not merged, deployed, claimed or owner-approved.

## Scope
Six existing directory records receive an editorial presentation, with original copy,
primary-source links, source-check dates and direct official website buttons. This is
not six paying customers or six partnerships. No outreach has been sent.

The homepage highlights this editorial collection. The directory uses the same
researched location and specialty information before filtering. All directory cards
now lead shoppers to the profile; the free-claim invitation is inside the profile.

No database data, ownership, billing tier, or verification timestamps are changed.
For the six selected slugs, the editorial page replaces the legacy display until
ownership_verified_at is present; reviewed owner-managed content then takes precedence.
Existing associated accounts get a management link instead of a new claim invitation.
This is a versioned editorial layer, not a long-term replacement for a CMS.

## Presentation and evidence decisions
- Neutral initials are typographic placeholders, not business logos. No third-party
  photographs were copied and no synthetic nursery imagery is presented as real.
- No ratings, testimonials, current stock, views, partner badges, memberships or
  quality guarantees are invented.
- Canopy's location is updated to Apopka based on its published May 2026 relocation.
- Orange Lake is shown at state level: its founding history does not establish a
  current public address. Do not assume its name means Orange Lake, FL.
- NSE is shown at state level with an explicit online-only/no-pickup note.
- Aroid Greenhouses' private farm is distinguished from Perfect Choice retail.
- Ecuagenera's event address is not represented as proof of normal opening hours.
- Owner correction requests can accompany free ownership-review submissions. A
  general public correction/removal intake remains a separate launch task; no
  unmonitored mailbox or nonworking form has been added.
