BECOME A VENDOR OR SPONSOR!


Are you interested in showcasing your plant-related business at the IAS Tropical Plant Expo? Join us as a vendor or sponsor (or both!) and get the opportunity to promote your products and services to plant enthusiasts, horticulturists, and industry professionals. Don’t miss this opportunity to grow your business!


The Expo is open to more than just aroids! Vendors selling live plants can bring aroids or other plants. Artists are able to bring what they have available that is plant-related.
