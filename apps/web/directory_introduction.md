I have created a new directory that lists rare plant vendors and rare plant community events across the world.

The directory serves the following purposes:

For Collectors (The buyers):

1. Connects collectors with trusted sellers of rare and exotic plants.
2. Collectors are normally forced to navigate a sea of unethical, misleading, or just plain fraudulent vendors. Our site only lists vendors that have been verified as "Trusted Vendors" through our proprietary deep research methods that leave no stone unturned. We deny a staggering 38% of vendors who apply to be verified on our site.
3. Collectors have access to our Events section, which lists rare plant events across the world and all the attending vendors, who have the opportunity to showcase their most desirable rare plant species and pricing.
4. Collectors can contact vendors, negotiate, and complete the purchase of plants prior to an event to ensure they don't lose a highly sought-after plant to another collector.  
5. Collectors can see detailed information through our proprietary CultivarID, which provides the complete lineage and caretaking history of the specific plant for sale, as well as an engaging education on the plant's history and care requirements.

For Vendors (The sellers):

1. Verified (Approved) vendors gain the credibility of being a trusted vendor, vetted by our proprietary vendor vetting process. This provides the much-needed credibility that is often lacking between vendors and collectors due to the fraud that has plagued this sector.  
2. Vendors can list their best selections prior to the event they will be attending. This gives collectors the chance to view and make an informed purchase, securing their acquisition and alleviating any fear of a scam.
3. Vendors get access to our CultivarID QR Coding application. With the push of a button, it creates a QR code that a collector can scan at an event on any single plant. This provides a complete overview of that exact plant's journey, lineage, and evolution, resulting in a fun, educational experience while eliminating any feeling of mistrust the collector may have of the vendor.
